The Jikajika Story

A Legacy of Innovation & Creative Mastery

Jika-jika Media (PTY) Ltd was established in 2018 in Philippi, Cape Town, under the leadership of Managing Director Zukisa Jika-jika. What began as a visionary photography and creative studio has expanded into a full-scale corporate production powerhouse handling multi-million rand summits, national business confederations, and high-profile institutional events.

We take immense pride in being a 100% black-owned, Level 1 B-BBEE contributor that actively creates high-skill technical employment in sound engineering, video broadcasting, lighting design, and digital media production across South Africa.

Guiding Principles

The Pillars of Our Corporate Standard

Technical Perfection

We deploy redundant audio lines, calibrated LED matrixes, and multi-camera 4K setups so that live events never suffer from technical hitches.

Client-Centric Integrity

We treat every corporate partner with direct communication, guaranteed on-time delivery, and full transparency throughout the production lifecycle.

Local Empowerment

As a 100% black-owned enterprise, we invest in young technical talent, offering mentorship in drone cinematography, sound engineering, and event lighting.
