Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book Audio Visual equipment?

We recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance for large corporate conferences and LED wall setups. However, we also cater to rapid emergency bookings subject to crew availability.

Do you travel outside Cape Town?

Yes! Jikajika Media regularly travels across South Africa (Johannesburg, Durban, Gqeberha, etc.) for corporate summits, brand films, and major institutional events.

What is the turnaround time for corporate event photos and videos?

We provide same-day social media preview clips and press photos during the event. Full edited 4K highlight videos and complete photo libraries are delivered within 48 to 72 hours.
